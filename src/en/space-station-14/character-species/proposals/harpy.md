## Overview

The harpy species is an avian species with greek mythological design and ideas such as being generally quick, having perfect mimickry, being able to sing very well, and are humanoid.
This document aims to present a general design suitable for Wizard's den.

## History of the species:

Harpies have been present since moth people existed (somewhere around 2017-2019) on SS13, and have been gradually ported to SS14 downstream forks.
The Harpy is a round-start species very common on downstream forks today (Goob, DeltaV, Monolith, Frontier), and have not seen much change design-wise in the past years.

## Design

Harpies are a lightweight, quick, and fragile bird-like species with slightly increased movement speed, brittle and hollow bones, and are very sensitive to atmospheric pollutants.
They are also able to mimick many sounds and voicelines, alongside being able to mimick a large variety of instruments for playing MIDIs.

### Core visual elements

Harpies are largely humanoid with human hair, have feather tufts on the side of their head, very large wings for arms, and digitigrade legs.
They are also smaller than other species on par with dwarves.
The color and specific avian theming of their wings can be customized with markings.

### Species Features

#### Vocal mimicry:

Harpies can perfectly mimick a very large part (~70%) of the sounds available in the game, but they cannot pick specific sound files to mimick. some examples are random gunshots, whirring, ringing, hissing, banging, barking, cawing, meowing, beeping, clicking... and so on.

#### Instrument mimickry and singing:

Harpies can sing and mimick almost any instrument at any time to play MIDI files.
making music causes colorful notes to dance around their head.

#### Flight:

Harpies have the ability to fly, which is an action that initiates a short do-after, after which gives them no gravity and increased speed/acceleration.
Flight costs stamina to use.

#### Inability to equip footwear:

Harpies have very different digitigrade feet which causes footwear of all kinds to be incompatible with them, footwear is never required anyway.

#### Brute vulnerability and increased speed:

The size of harpies and their hollow bones cause them to have increased vulnerability to brute damage, but be slightly faster than other species.

#### Increased atmospheric sensitivity:

Harpies are very biologically similar to birds, and so their lungs take in air during inhalation and exhalation.
This causes them to be very sensitive to ammonia, carbon dioxide, and pressure changes.
